Brian's 29er adventure bike is a steel frame with a curved top tube and a custom built unicrown steel fork to match. We used low mount paragon disc dropouts that were carved out a bit to lighten them up and make them special. The bike includes a custom steel stem to match. The XT components ensure the bike will be a smooth operator.
